Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EPlate (“the Bot”) handles information when you use it on Discord. EPlate is a Discord bot that provides virtual license-plate rolling and collection features, including balances, garages, profiles, upgrades and leaderboards.

1. Information we process

To provide the Bot’s features, EPlate may process and store:

  • Discord user IDs, used to associate game progress with a Discord account;
  • Discord server (guild) IDs, used to keep server game data and settings separate;
  • game data, such as virtual balances, collected license plates, garage contents, progress and related game statistics;
  • Bot configuration, such as selected command channels, logging channel settings and configured administrator roles;
  • command and operational information needed to respond to commands, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain the Bot.

When prefix commands are enabled, Discord message content is processed to recognize commands such as !крутить. EPlate is not intended to archive ordinary conversations, and ordinary message content is not intentionally stored as game data.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the Bot, save and restore game progress, provide requested commands, maintain server-specific settings, enforce administrator permissions, protect the service against abuse and diagnose technical problems.

3. Storage and service providers

Game data and settings are stored in the Bot’s database. Operational logs may also be created to diagnose errors and record relevant Bot activity. The Bot communicates with Discord to provide its features. The Bot is hosted using a third-party hosting provider; that provider may process information as needed to run and maintain the Bot.

We do not sell personal information or use EPlate game data for targeted advertising.

4. Sharing

Information is not publicly disclosed by EPlate except where a feature intentionally displays game information (for example, profiles or leaderboards), where disclosure is requested by you, or where disclosure is necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or protect the Bot and its users.

5. Retention and deletion requests

Game data is retained while it is needed to provide EPlate. Server administrators may have access to server-level reset controls. If you want to ask about access to or deletion of data associated with your Discord account, contact the EPlate team through the official support server. Please provide your Discord user ID and, where relevant, the server involved. We may need to verify the request before taking action.

Some operational records may remain for a limited period where needed for security, troubleshooting or legal obligations.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the Bot’s database and operational environment. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children

You must use Discord and EPlate in accordance with Discord’s terms and the laws that apply to you. EPlate is not designed to knowingly collect information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes

This Policy may be updated when EPlate’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the EPlate team in the EPlate support Discord server.
